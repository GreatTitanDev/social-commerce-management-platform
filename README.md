# Social Commerce Platform

## Project Vision
Create a social commerce platform that helps creators and small businesses turn community engagement into measurable sales through a simple, trustworthy, and highly useful product experience.

## Mission
Build a product that makes discovery, trust, and purchase decisions easier for customers while giving creators and merchants a sustainable way to grow.

## Current Stage
Research and planning. This repository captures product thinking, customer insights, early architecture decisions, and execution plans before development begins.

## Repository Purpose
This repository serves as the company’s working memory. It is designed to organize the startup’s ideas, research, validation work, product direction, technical planning, and launch preparation in one place.

## Repository Structure
- research/: market research, competitor analysis, and customer interview notes
- product/: product strategy, roadmap, requirements, and backlog materials
- architecture/: technical decisions, API planning, database design, and system architecture
- design/: product and UI direction notes
- development-planning/: delivery planning, milestones, and execution notes
- branding/: brand positioning and communication direction
- meeting-notes/: decisions and discussion summaries
- assets/: imagery, diagrams, and supporting files
- backend/, frontend/, infrastructure/, scripts/, docs/: future implementation and operational space

## Startup Principles
- Start with real customer problems, not assumptions.
- Keep the first product simple, useful, and testable.
- Make decisions that can be revised quickly as learning grows.
- Document what matters so the team can move with clarity.
- Build the next step, not the entire future at once.

## Development Philosophy
- Prioritize clarity over complexity.
- Validate ideas through customer feedback before scaling investment.
- Keep architecture practical and flexible.
- Favor small, measurable milestones over large, risky bets.
- Maintain documentation as the product evolves.

## Long-Term Goals
- Validate a clear problem and target audience.
- Define an MVP that solves the highest-value pain point.
- Establish a strong product and technical foundation.
- Launch, learn, and improve with disciplined iteration.

## Roadmap Overview
1. Research and problem validation
2. Product definition and MVP scope
3. Technical planning and architecture decisions
4. Development execution and internal validation
5. Launch preparation and customer rollout
